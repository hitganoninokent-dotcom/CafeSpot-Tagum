      Privacy Policy

      Last updated: October 19, 2026

      CafeSpot Tagum ("we", "us") respects your privacy and follows the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). This page explains what we collect and why.

      What we collect

      • Account details you give us: name, email, handle, and a hashed (never plain-text) password and recovery key.
      • Your sign-in session. With “Keep me logged in” it stays on this device for 30 days; without it, it ends when you close the browser. Accounts kept logged in are listed on this device's log-in screen until you remove them.
      • Your location, only if you allow it, to show distances to cafes. It is processed in your browser and is not uploaded or stored.
      • Login activity (time, account and device type) for security auditing. Administrators only see it with names, handles and emails partly masked.
      • Saved and visited cafes, reviews you post, badges you earn and a history of these on your dashboard.
      • Usage events (pages viewed, searches, logins) only if you accept analytics cookies.

      Where it is stored

      In this version, all data is stored in your own browser's local storage. Clearing your browser data removes it.

      Your rights

      You may access, correct or delete your data at any time. Decline analytics in the cookie banner to stop event logging, or contact us to request deletion.

      Contact

      Data protection questions: reach us through our social handle @cafespot.tagum.
